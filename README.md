## About the series👀
**The 100** is an American science fiction and post-apocalyptic television series created by **Jason Rothenberg**.It premiered on March 19, 2014, and has seven seasons.

### Storyline📝
The story takes place 97 years after a nuclear apocalypse destroyed the Earth. One hundred young prisoners are sent from a space station called the Ark to check if the Earth is safe to live in again.

They must face different challenges, dangerous enemies, and difficult decisions to survive.

## Main Characters👥✨
- **Clarke Griffin** - A brave and intelligent leader.
- **Bellamy Blake** – Protective older brother, co-leader of the delinquents.
- **Octavia Blake** – Bellamy’s sister, evolves into a warrior and later a major political figure.
- **Raven Reyes** – Engineer and mechanic, key problem-solver for the group.
- **John Murphy** – Initially antagonistic delinquent who becomes a complex survivor.
- **Finn Collins** – Tracker and early love interest of Clarke.
- **Jasper Jordan** – Chemist and gunner, Monty’s best friend.
- **Monty Green** – Engineer and agriculturist, Jasper’s best friend.
- **Wells Jaha** – Son of the Chancellor, Clarke’s former best friend.

## Why I love The 100❤️
I love this series because of its interesting storyline, unexpected plot twists, and amazing characters. It teaches lessons about friendship, survival, leadership, and sacrifice.
My favorite part is seeing how the characters change and grow through the series. 

## Favorite Quote🪶

This is one of the most memorable phrases in the series.

## My Rating⭐

## About the Series👀

**Breaking Bad** is an American crime drama television series created by **Vince Gilligan**. It premiered on January 20, 2008, and has five seasons.

### Storyline📝

The story follows Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher who discovers that he has cancer. Worried about his family's financial future, he starts making and selling illegal drugs with his former student, Jesse Pinkman.

As time passes, Walter becomes involved in the dangerous world of crime, leading to unexpected consequences.

## Main Characters👥

- **Walter White** – A chemistry teacher who becomes a drug kingpin.

- **Jesse Pinkman** – Walter's former student and business partner.

- **Skyler White** – Walter's wife.

- **Hank Schrader** – Walter's brother-in-law and a DEA agent.

- **Saul Goodman** – A lawyer who helps Walter and Jesse.

- **Gustavo Fring** – A powerful drug distributor.

## Why I Love Breaking Bad❤️

I love this series because of its amazing storyline, intense scenes, and unexpected plot twists. I find Walter White's character development interesting because it shows how a person's decisions can completely change their life. It also makes me think about how greed, ambition, and power can affect a person.

## Favorite Quote🪶

## About the Series👀
**Never Have I Ever** is an American teen comedy-drama series created by **Mindy Kaling and Lang Fisher**. It premiered on Netflix on April 27, 2020, and has four seasons.

### Storyline📝
The story follows Devi Vishwakumar, an Indian-American high school student who wants to improve her social life after experiencing a difficult year. She deals with friendship problems, family expectations, school pressure, and complicated romantic relationships.

Throughout the series, Devi learns about love, friendship, family, and accepting herself.

## Main Characters👥
- **Devi Vishwakumar** – An ambitious and emotional teenager.
- **Ben Gross** – Devi's intelligent classmate and rival.
- **Paxton Hall-Yoshida** – A popular student and Devi's love interest.
- **Eleanor Wong** – One of Devi's best friends who loves acting.
- **Fabiola Torres** – Devi's smart and supportive best friend.
- **Kamala** – Devi's cousin who faces family and relationship expectations.

## Why I Love Never Have I Ever❤️
I love this series because it shows the reality of being a teenager, including friendship, love, family problems, and personal struggles. I enjoy how funny and relatable the characters are. It also teaches us that growing up means making mistakes, learning from them, and accepting ourselves.

## Favorite Quote🪶
> "Sometimes growing up means accepting that things won't always turn out the way you imagined."

## My Rating⭐
**10/10** – A fun and relatable series!
